# Expense Reimbursement Process

## Overview
This guide outlines the process for submitting and getting reimbursed for business-related expenses incurred by employees.

## Eligible Expenses
- Travel expenses (flights, trains, taxis, fuel) for business trips
- Accommodation during business travel
- Client entertainment (meals, events) with prior approval
- Office supplies purchased with prior authorization

## Non-Eligible Expenses
- Personal meals or entertainment
- Alcohol (unless pre-approved for client entertainment)
- Expenses without receipts

## Submission Process
1. Log in to the Finance portal at `https://finance.company.com/expenses`.
2. Click **New Expense Report** and enter the date, category, and amount.
3. Upload scanned receipts or photos for each expense item.
4. Add a brief business justification.
5. Submit the report for manager approval.

## Approval & Payment
- Manager reviews and approves within 3 business days.
- Finance team processes approved claims within 5 working days.
- Reimbursement is made via direct bank transfer to your registered account.

## Limits
- Meals: Up to $50 per day without pre-approval.
- Hotel: Up to $150 per night without pre-approval.
- Amounts exceeding these limits require prior written approval.

For questions, email finance@company.com.
