# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Welcome to the Team!
This guide will help you get set up and ready on your first day and during your first week.

## Day 1 Checklist
- [ ] Receive company laptop and accessories from IT
- [ ] Set up your company email account
- [ ] Change your initial network password (see Password Reset guide)
- [ ] Install the VPN client and connect (see VPN Setup guide)
- [ ] Download company communication tools: Slack and Microsoft Teams
- [ ] Complete the security awareness training (mandatory, link in your welcome email)

## Account Setup
- **Company Email**: Provided by IT — format is `firstname.lastname@company.com`
- **HR Portal**: `https://hr.company.com` — manage leave and personal details
- **IT Portal**: `https://it.company.com` — request hardware and software
- **Finance Portal**: `https://finance.company.com` — expense submissions

## First Week
- Meet your line manager and team members
- Review your role's 30-60-90 day plan shared by your manager
- Enroll in the mandatory compliance training by the end of week 1
- Set up payroll details via HR portal under **Payroll Settings**
