# Laptop Request Process

## Overview
This document explains how employees can request a new laptop or hardware replacement through the IT department.

## When to Submit a Request
- New employee joining who hasn't received equipment yet.
- Your current laptop is more than 4 years old and significantly underperforming.
- Hardware failure or irreparable damage.
- Role change requiring different hardware specifications.

## How to Submit a Request
1. Log in to the IT portal at `https://it.company.com/requests`.
2. Select **Hardware Request** → **Laptop Request**.
3. Fill in the request form:
   - Current device details (model, serial number, age)
   - Reason for request
   - Required specifications (if any specific software requirements)
4. Your manager's approval is required — the system automatically sends them a notification.
5. After manager approval, IT will review availability and raise a procurement order if needed.

## Processing Time
- In-stock models: 3–5 business days.
- Procurement required: 2–4 weeks depending on availability.

## Device Return Policy
- Old laptops must be returned to IT within 5 days of receiving the replacement.
- IT will wipe and redeploy or retire the old device.

For urgent hardware issues, contact IT at it@company.com or ext. 5678.
