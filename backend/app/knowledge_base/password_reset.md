# Password Reset Policy

## Overview
This document outlines the process for resetting your company account password when you are locked out or need to change it for security reasons.

## When to Reset Your Password
- You have forgotten your current password.
- Your account has been locked after multiple failed login attempts.
- You receive a security alert requiring an immediate password change.
- Regular rotation (passwords must be changed every 90 days).

## Self-Service Reset (Recommended)
1. Navigate to the IT portal at `https://portal.company.com/reset`.
2. Enter your company email address.
3. Click **Send Reset Link**. A reset link will be emailed to your registered personal email.
4. Open the email and click the link (valid for 30 minutes).
5. Create a new password following the complexity requirements below.

## Password Requirements
- Minimum 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character
- Cannot reuse the last 5 passwords

## Contact IT Support
If the self-service portal does not work, contact IT support at support@company.com or call the helpdesk at ext. 1234.
