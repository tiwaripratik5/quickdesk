# VPN Setup Guide

## Overview
This guide explains how to set up and connect to the company VPN (Virtual Private Network) on your device.

## Prerequisites
- Company-issued credentials (username and password)
- VPN client software (provided by IT during onboarding)
- Internet connection

## Installation Steps
1. Download the VPN client from the company intranet portal at `https://intranet.company.com/vpn`.
2. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions.
3. Once installed, open the VPN client application.

## Connecting to the VPN
1. Launch the VPN client from your taskbar or applications menu.
2. Enter the server address: `vpn.company.com`
3. Use your company email as the username and your network password.
4. Click **Connect** and wait for the connection to establish.
5. A green indicator confirms a successful connection.

## Troubleshooting
- **Cannot connect**: Ensure your internet is working and the server address is correct.
- **Authentication failed**: Reset your network password via the IT portal.
- **Slow connection**: Switch between VPN server regions in the client settings.
- **VPN disconnects frequently**: Check your firewall settings and whitelist the VPN client.

For further assistance, contact the IT helpdesk at support@company.com or open a ticket.
