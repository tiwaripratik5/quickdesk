# Leave Application Process

## Overview
This guide explains how employees can apply for different types of leave using the company's HR portal.

## Types of Leave
- **Annual Leave**: Paid time off accrued monthly (1.5 days per month).
- **Sick Leave**: Up to 12 days per calendar year, no carry-over.
- **Casual Leave**: 6 days per year for personal or urgent matters.
- **Maternity/Paternity Leave**: As per local regulations.

## How to Apply
1. Log in to the HR portal at `https://hr.company.com`.
2. Navigate to **Leave Management** → **Apply for Leave**.
3. Select the leave type, start date, and end date.
4. Provide a brief reason in the remarks field.
5. Upload any required documentation (e.g., medical certificate for sick leave > 2 days).
6. Click **Submit**. Your manager will receive an email for approval.

## Approval Process
- Your line manager will approve or reject the leave within 2 business days.
- You will receive an email notification with the decision.
- Approved leave is automatically reflected in the system.

## Leave Balance
View your current leave balances on the HR portal under **My Leave Summary**.

For HR-related queries, contact hr@company.com.
